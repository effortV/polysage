# Effects of different compatibilizers on the rheological, thermomechanical, and m

- 类型：literature
- 来源：Effects of different compatibilizers on the rheological, thermomechanical, and morphological properties of HDPE/LLDPE blend‐based nanocomposites（crossref，2013）https://doi.org/10.1002/app.39265

## 出处
Fabio Roberto Passador, Adhemar Collà Ruvolo‐Filho, Luiz Antonio Pessan, Journal of Applied Polymer Science, 2013, 10.1002/app.39265

## 研究体系
HDPE/LLDPE blend-based nanocomposites with organoclay; compatibilizers: HDPE-g-MA, LLDPE-g-MA, and their 50/50 mixture; nanoclay content: 2.5 wt%; preparation: melt-intercalation in torque rheometer; film thickness: 资料未提供

## 结论（只记与本项目有关的）
- LLDPE-g-MA with high melt index (low viscosity and crystallinity) is an effective compatibilizer for HDPE/LLDPE blend-based nanocomposites (定性)
- Compatibilized nanocomposites with LLDPE-g-MA or mixture of HDPE-g-MA and LLDPE-g-MA exhibited better nanoclay dispersion and distribution with stronger matrix-nanoclay interactions (定性)
- Addition of maleic anhydride grafted polyethylene facilitates exfoliation and/or intercalation of clays and its adhesion to HDPE/LLDPE blend (定性)
